### Red (and white)
Red corgis are the easiest to identify in my opinion, they have a grayish-red coat when they are born, and it will turn into a red color as they grow up.
<img class="img" src="/assets/images/welpen/poppy.jpg" alt="Red Corgi" /> (4 Days)

I think even the untrained eye can now see that closest to me taking the picture is a red puppy. The others appear completely black. 
But be careful! Red can range from a light tan to a deep red.
Intensly colored puppys tend to keep their intense color but the grayish puppys can also develop very intense coloring.

Here you can see the progression of a red (or more so sable, more on that later on) girl.

### Tricolor
Tricolor is a tricky thing and as you saw. There are read headed and black headed tricolors.
#### Example here

They are very hard to distinguish at birth but around X weeks of age they start to show color at the inner tip of their ear.
When there is a black tip it's a black headed tricolor when its whitish-red its a redheaded.
Unfortunately there is no way to separate the black head factored reds from the purely red headed puppys before a lot older of age.
