## Introduction
Nice that you found my blog! Today I will be talking about how to tell how a coat color will develop in a corgis life.

This question came up at my recent vet visit when my vet asked me exactly the question im trying to answer here: What color will that puppy have 
For that we will have a look wht colors do exist and how they look as a puppy, a young dog and as an adult.

### Red (and white)
Red corgis are the easiest to identify in my opinion, they have a grayish coat when they are born, and it will turn into a red color as they grow up.
<img class="img" src="/assets/images/welpen/poppy.jpg" alt="Red Corgi" />
I think even the untrained eye can now see that closest to me taking the picture is a red puppy. The others appear completely black. 
But be careful! Red can range from a light tan to a deep red.

This one developed into a wonderful red girl.
